Statue 1 climb

Works · 1-frame tornado

Start: entering pra_18 from the west door (53, −70) with Ultra Boots. No partner tricks; Lakilester just follows.

Inputs

  1. Hold → until Mario stops against the statue's face.
  2. Hold ↑ until he stops in the corner with the north wall.
  3. Hold ↓ and run south along the face.
  4. Press A on run frame 7 or 8 and hold it 6–7 frames. Keep holding ↓.
  5. Tornado: press A again exactly 8 frames after the first A, and switch the stick to → (or let go) on that press.
  6. Mario lands on the club and stays there. Take your time.
  7. A + →: he jumps over the statue top and runs down the corridor to statue 2.

Statue 2 climb

Easy

Start: in the corridor after statue 1, Mario running into statue 2's face at (437, −77.6).

Inputs

  1. Hold ↓ into the corridor's bottom-right corner.
  2. Hold ↗. Mario runs north and pins on the corridor's north wall.
  3. Press A any time from frame 7 on and hold it 5–9 frames.
  4. Tornado: A 6–10 frames after the first A, with ↗ or → still held. A zeroed stick fails.
  5. Mario lands on the club.
  6. A + →: over statue 2 and into the east section.

South → north mirror clip

Works · 1-frame C-down

Start: south side of the mirror (z > 0) in the east section, riding Lakilester. Tested with the east Clubba at (717, 70) already gone; it blocks the ride otherwise.

Inputs

  1. Ride diagonally into the east wall and let go. Laki settles at x 732.0.
  2. Hold ← exactly 4 frames (x 708).
  3. Hold ↑ into the mirror for 45–80 frames (about 1.5–2.7 s). Laki slides west slowly.
  4. Tap ↓ for 1–4 frames.
  5. C▼ to get off with the stick neutral.
  6. Start holding ← any time during the dismount (frames 1–15 after the get-off press).
  7. C▼ on frame 34 after the get-off press, with ← still held.

  • Mario on the C▼ framex 615.6 (611 = late, 620 = early)

Why the up-hold: Laki's speed spike always lands on frame 34, and with left held Mario's walk is off by about 1.5 units unless Laki gets off at x 706.1–706.9. Holding up drifts Laki west 0.023 units per frame because the camera is slightly rotated. No wall in the room gives that x with a fixed stop.

North → south mirror clip (right side)

Research only

Start: north side, NE pocket north of statue 3, with Speedy Spin equipped. Not yet runner-viable: the setup freezes Laki's 40-entry follow history and then pushes him along the north wall with a steered, touching push that uses analog angles every frame.

Setup outline

  1. Mount, ride into the NE corner, get off; walk out west and back along the north wall into the corner.
  2. Small up/down taps beside Laki until his history fills and freezes (trail ends near (733, −120)).
  3. Touching push west along the north wall, around the bump: about 180°×250, 240°×120, 180°×250 frames. Leaves Laki stuck near (517.5, −132.6).
  4. Hold ← along the wall: Mario stops at x 513.00.
