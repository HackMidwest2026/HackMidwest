pra_02 reverse mirror clip: speeds that work

Top-down view of the back side of the first palace room, mirror at the bottom. Each 5×5 cell is a spot where Mario stood. The game was told Laki's speed was stuck at S, then C-down was pressed. A cell is coloured if Mario ended up on the front side of the mirror.

Thin strip

Against the right wall at x ≈ 980, z −15 to −65, the clip works from 170. That's the cheapest spot on the map.

Right of the second pillar

From x 750 to the right wall, the clip works from 220 almost everywhere: the top-right corner, the open floor and the top of the switch block. None of these spots stops clipping at higher speeds, up to 320.

Under the second pillar

From x 660 to 740, the speed needed climbs by about 10 for every 10 units you move left, from 250 to 320. At x 740 it only works at exactly 290.

Left of x 660

No speed from 20 to 320 clips anywhere on the left two-thirds of the room.

Speed must stay put

The test holds Laki's speed at S on every frame until Mario mounts, like the frozen speed when Laki is stuck. Setting it once gets overwritten and never clips.
