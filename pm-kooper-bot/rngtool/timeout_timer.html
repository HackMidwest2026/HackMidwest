In the run

  1. Watch somewhere quiet. Mario's first battle menu, or standing in the last staircase room before the hallway (C-up shows the status bar; not on the same frame as a spin). Tap Shine on every white flash. Press Find seed after about 13 shines; keep tapping if it reports more than one seed.
  2. Gap. Before leaving to do something else (turn 1, the hallway door), you can stop tapping. When the coin is in view again, press New segment, then tap again. A few shines resync it; the page shows when it is locked.
  3. Turn 2. Open Star Spirits, up 3 to Time Out, A, so the hand is on Bowser. Keep tapping shines while you do this if you can.
  4. Press. Press Plan Time Out. Confirm Bowser with A on the last beep. The strip shows the window you are aiming at.

Shines come 48 frames after a shine and 24 after a normal spin; one game frame is 33.4 ms. The Time Out roll comes 386 frames after the confirm and works on 30 or less, a 30.7% chance on a random frame. Verified frame for frame in an emulator, including after Super Jump Charge, Spiny Flip and Bowser's first attack.
