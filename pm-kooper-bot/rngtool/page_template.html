Watch Seed Window

Tap Shine each time the status-bar coin flashes white.

Normal spins fill themselves in from the timing between shines. After about 13 shines (45 seconds) press Find seed.

Timing settings

In the run (automatic)

  1. Once, before runs. Save for OBS mode and open that file in Chrome or Edge. In OBS, right-click your game capture source and choose Windowed Projector (Source); leave that window open (it can sit behind other windows, but not minimized). Here, keep the source on "A window", press Start watching and pick the projector window. Drag the gold box tightly around the status-bar coin. Go to Time Out's target screen (hand on Bowser), click Time Out's Hand on Hallway, drag the blue box over the クッパ name box in the top-left corner, and press Save target screen. The boxes and the target screen are remembered.
  2. Calibrate (a few practice presses). After each confirm, Press check under the strip reads from the video which frame you actually pressed on, measured against the middle of the window. After 3 presses that agree, press Move beeps if you pressed on the beeps, or Move visual if you pressed when the gold line reached the now-line. The two are separate because sound and picture reach you with different delays.
  3. During the run, leave the page watching. Whenever the coin is on screen it reads the spins, finds the seed after about 13 shines, and resyncs by itself after every room transition, pause or turn.
  4. Turn 2. Go to Time Out's target screen. The page sees it, picks a window and starts beeping. Press A on the last (higher) beep. If you leave the screen early the beeps stop.

The OBS Virtual Camera also works as a source (start it in OBS first). The page only ever keeps the OBS camera open, never a capture card that OBS is using. The seed search uses 2 threads by default so OBS keeps its CPU; it takes about 20 s.

Without a camera (tapping)

  1. Watch somewhere quiet. Mario's first battle menu, or standing in the last staircase room before the hallway (C-up shows the status bar; not on the same frame as a spin). Tap Shine on every white flash. Press Find seed after about 13 shines; keep tapping if it reports more than one seed.
  2. Gap. Before leaving to do something else (turn 1, the hallway door), you can stop tapping. When the coin is in view again, press New segment, then tap again. A few shines resync it; the page shows when it is locked.
  3. Turn 2. Open Star Spirits, up 3 to Time Out, A, so the hand is on Bowser. Keep tapping shines while you do this if you can.
  4. Press. Press Plan Time Out. Confirm Bowser with A on the last beep. The strip shows the window you are aiming at.

Shines come 48 frames after a shine and 24 after a normal spin; one game frame is 33.4 ms. The Time Out roll comes 386 frames after the confirm and works on 30 or less, a 30.7% chance on a random frame. Verified frame for frame in an emulator, including after Super Jump Charge, Spiny Flip and Bowser's first attack.
