Gold S = shine you tapped, dot = normal spin inferred from the gap.

  1. Watch somewhere quiet. Mario's first battle menu, or standing in the last staircase room before the hallway (C-up shows the status bar; not on the same frame as a spin). Tap Shine on every white flash. Press Find seed after about 13 shines; keep tapping if it reports more than one seed.
  2. Gap. Before leaving to do something else (turn 1, the hallway door), you can stop tapping. When the coin is in view again, press New segment, then tap again. A few shines resync it; the page shows when it is locked.
  3. Turn 2. Open Star Spirits, up 3 to Time Out, A, so the hand is on Bowser. Keep tapping shines while you do this if you can.
  4. Press. Press Plan press. Confirm Bowser with A on the last beep. The strip shows the frame you are aiming at.

In the run (automatic)

  1. Once, before runs. Save for OBS mode and open that file in Chrome or Edge. In OBS, right-click your game capture source and choose Windowed Projector (Source); leave that window open (it can sit behind other windows, but not minimized). Here, keep the source on "A window", press Start watching and pick the projector window. Drag the gold box tightly around the status-bar coin. Go to Time Out's target screen (hand on Bowser), click Time Out's Hand on Hallway, drag the blue box over the クッパ name box in the top-left corner, and press Save target screen. The boxes and the target screen are remembered.
  2. Calibrate (a few practice presses). After each confirm, Press check under the strip reads from the video which frame you actually pressed on, against the aimed frame. Once it agrees over a few presses, open Calibrate… under the strip, check what it will change and confirm: it moves the beep offset and the visual offset by the measured error. Pick Red (one exact frame) as the window to test the timing; Green (3+) for runs.
  3. During the run, leave the page watching. Whenever the coin is on screen it reads the spins, finds the seed after about 13 shines, and resyncs by itself after every room transition, pause or turn.
  4. Turn 2. Go to Time Out's target screen. The page sees it, picks a window of the kind set under Settings and plays exactly the set number of beeps. Press A on the last (higher) beep, or when the gold aim line reaches the now-line. Backing out of the target screen (B) is not counted as a press; the beeps stop and start again when you come back.

The OBS Virtual Camera also works as a source (start it in OBS first). The page only ever keeps the OBS camera open, never a capture card that OBS is using. The seed search uses 2 threads by default so OBS keeps its CPU; it takes about 20 s.

Shines come 48 frames after a shine and 24 after a normal spin; one game frame is 33.4 ms. The Time Out roll comes 386 frames after the confirm and works on 30 or less, a 30.7% chance on a random frame. Verified frame for frame in an emulator, including after Super Jump Charge, Spiny Flip and Bowser's first attack.

Endgame route

Duplighosts → Jr. Troopa → Hallway Bowser → Final Bowser · click a numbered step to jump there · ★ = planned press

Zero-wait route: never stop for the page.

  1. No coin is needed on the walks: each fight's Super Charge reads the seed.
  2. Duplighosts on the Charge-lock route: Mario's Super Charge first (the page reads the seed from its rays), then keep the C-up bar up in Bombette's menu: its shines decide between the possible first coin frames. Do Nothing on the beep.
  3. Jr. Troopa: Mario's Super Charge first on T1 (its rays lock the seed; the T3 and T5 Charges check it). The page counts your confirms from the status bar hiding and moves the route by itself; it beeps the T3 Bomb, the T4 Maple Syrup, the T5 Do Nothing (save Bombette's Strategies screen once: its closing is the confirm) and the T6 Power Bounce. It reads Mario's HP after each Close Call: after a Life Shroom the next turn has no roll, and the next Charge locks again. No lock: play it blind (Close Call 30%; Power Bounce 5+ 67.5%, 6+ 32%).
  4. Hallway Bowser T1: Mario's Charge locks the seed by itself. The coin in the T1 and T2 menus only confirms it and tells apart what the Spiny Flip and Bowser's attack left open.
  5. Time Out's target screen: the page beeps its best frame and shows the chance it works, or says no edge, press normally. Following a beep is the only time it adds: the lead plus up to the max extra wait (Settings, Advanced), about 1-1.5 s.
  6. Final Bowser T1-T4: Mario's Super Charge on T1 locks the seed (T2 and T4's Charges check it). Wait with the hand on Mario for Cloud Nine (T1), then on Lakilester's Do Nothing (T2-T4), and confirm on the beep: Bowser misses (T4: the wave comes; block it, Laki may take it). Do not press A while Cloud Nine's message prints (only its three action presses). Peach Beam on T5 as usual: the seed it carries makes the T6 Charge lock fast. No lock: play it blind (about 50% a turn, 39% on T4).
With no lock, or when the coin no longer fits the lock (doubtful), the page says no edge and you press normally (31%). Numbers: charge_live/README.md.
